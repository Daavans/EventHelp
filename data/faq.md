# Riverside Festival — Frequently Asked Questions

Riverside Festival is a three-day music festival on the banks of the river Waal in Tiel,
the Netherlands. This FAQ is fictional and used as the knowledge base for the Event Assistant.

## When does Riverside Festival take place?
Riverside Festival takes place from Friday 16 July to Sunday 18 July 2027 at the Waalstrand
in Tiel. The gates open at 12:00 every day. The music ends at 23:00 on Friday and Saturday,
and at 22:00 on Sunday. The festival grounds close one hour after the last show.

## Who is playing at the festival?
The full line-up is published on our website. Headliners are The Lowland Echoes on Friday,
Marlow & The Tides on Saturday and DJ Nova Waal on Sunday. There are three stages: the
Main Stage, the Harbour Tent and the small Willow Stage for upcoming local artists.
Set times are announced in the festival app one week before the festival.

## What types of tickets are there and what do they cost?
There are three ticket types:
- Day ticket: €65, valid for one festival day of your choice.
- Weekend ticket: €149, valid for all three days.
- Camping ticket: €189, a weekend ticket including a spot on the campsite from Thursday 15 July.
Weekend tickets and camping tickets are sold out. You can join the waitlist; when tickets
become available, people on the waitlist are contacted in order of registration.

## How do I get my ticket and can I transfer it?
Your ticket is a personal e-ticket with a QR code, sent by email after purchase. Show the
QR code on your phone at the entrance. You can transfer your ticket to someone else for free
via the official ticket shop until 48 hours before the festival. Tickets bought from
unofficial resellers are not guaranteed to be valid.

## Is there an age limit?
The minimum age is 16. Visitors aged 16 or 17 must be able to show a valid ID at the entrance
and receive a wristband that does not allow them to buy alcohol. Children under 16 are not
allowed, even when accompanied by an adult.

## Where can I park?
Parking is available at P-Waalstrand, a 10-minute walk from the entrance. A parking ticket
costs €15 per day and must be bought online in advance; there is no payment at the gate.
The number of parking spots is limited. There is a separate drop-off zone at the
Industrieweg for people picking up or dropping off visitors.

## How do I get there by public transport?
Tiel railway station is a 20-minute walk from the festival. During the festival a free
shuttle bus runs between Tiel station and the festival entrance every 10 minutes, from 11:30
until one hour after the last show. Extra late-night trains run to Utrecht and Nijmegen on
Friday and Saturday.

## Can I come by bike?
Yes. There is a free, guarded bicycle parking at the main entrance, open from 11:00 until
one hour after the festival grounds close. Bikes left overnight are removed.

## What are the camping facilities?
The campsite opens on Thursday 15 July at 14:00 and closes on Monday 19 July at 12:00.
It has free showers, toilets, phone charging points and a small supermarket. You bring your
own tent. Campfires, gas barbecues and generators are not allowed on the campsite.
Quiet hours on the campsite are from 02:00 to 08:00.

## What am I allowed to bring?
You may bring a small bag (maximum A4 size), an empty reusable water bottle (maximum 0.5 litre),
sunscreen, a power bank, earplugs and prescribed medication with a doctor's note.

## What am I not allowed to bring?
Not allowed: glass, cans, your own food and drinks, drugs, weapons, fireworks, umbrellas,
professional cameras, drones, laser pointers and large flags with poles.
There are security checks at the entrance. Prohibited items are confiscated and not returned.

## Is there food and drink at the festival?
Yes, there are more than 25 food stands with vegetarian, vegan, halal and gluten-free options.
Drinking water is free at several water points across the festival grounds.

## How do I pay at the festival?
Riverside Festival is cashless. You pay with tokens, which you buy with a debit card, credit
card or mobile phone at the token machines or in the festival app. One token costs €3.
A soft drink costs 1 token and a beer costs 1.5 tokens. Cash is not accepted.

## Can I get a refund for unused tokens?
Yes. Unused digital tokens are refunded automatically to your bank account within 14 days
after the festival, minus a €1 administration fee. Physical tokens cannot be refunded.

## Is the festival accessible for people with a disability?
Yes. All festival grounds are wheelchair accessible with hard paths. There are accessible
toilets near every stage and a raised viewing platform at the Main Stage. Visitors with a
disability can request a free companion ticket via the customer service; the request must be
made at least two weeks before the festival.

## Is there first aid at the festival?
Yes. The first aid (EHBO) post is located next to the Harbour Tent and is staffed by medical
professionals during all opening hours. In an emergency, tell the nearest staff member or
security guard, or call 112.

## Are there lockers?
Yes, lockers are available next to the main entrance. A small locker costs €10 per day and
a large locker €15 per day. Lockers can be reserved in advance in the festival app; the
remaining lockers are rented on site on a first-come, first-served basis. Lockers have a
charging point for your phone.

## What happens if it rains?
The festival continues in rain. The Harbour Tent and the Willow Stage are covered. Umbrellas
are not allowed, so bring a rain poncho. In case of a thunderstorm or dangerous weather the
organisation may pause the programme; follow the instructions of staff and the festival app.

## What should I do if I lose something?
Go to the Info Point at the main entrance during the festival. After the festival, lost and
found items can be requested via the customer service until 31 August 2027.

## Is there a festival app?
Yes, the free Riverside Festival app shows the line-up, set times, a map of the festival
grounds, your tokens and important announcements. It is available for iOS and Android.

## How do I contact customer service?
You can reach customer service by email at info@riversidefestival.example or via the chat in
the festival app. Customer service answers within two working days. During the festival the
Info Point at the main entrance is open during all opening hours.
